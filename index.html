Welcome to Korgi Kode! Privacy Policy for Clara’s Home Inventory Effective Date: August 5, 2026 Korgi Kode (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Clara’s Home Inventory handles your information. Information We Collect Clara’s Home Inventory is designed to help you organize and catalog your household belongings. The app stores the information you enter, such as: * Storage box information * Item names and descriptions * Categories * Notes * Photos you choose to attach to items This information is stored locally on your Mac. Camera Access If you choose to use the QR code scanning feature, the app requests permission to access your camera. Camera access is used solely to scan QR codes and is never used for any other purpose. Photos If you choose to attach photos to inventory items, the app requests access to your photo library only when needed. Photos are used only within the app and remain under your control. Data Sharing Clara’s Home Inventory does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Internet Connectivity The app does not require an online account to function. Your inventory data is not transmitted to Korgi Kode. Data Security Your information remains stored on your device. You are responsible for maintaining backups of your data using your preferred backup solution. Children’s Privacy Clara’s Home Inventory is not directed toward children under the age of 13. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.
